Federated ontologies

VsML is designed to federate across:

  • OCX OCX Schema — structural and dimensional vessel data
  • SFI SFI Index System — ship systems reference designators
  • ORG W3C Organization Ontology — organizational concepts
  • PROV PROV-O — provenance and documentation
  • OM Ontology of Units of Measure — measurements and units
